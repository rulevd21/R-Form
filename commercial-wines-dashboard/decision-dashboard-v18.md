# Decision Dashboard v18

The v18 layer converts the analytical report into a commercial decision dashboard.

## Executive summary
Six top-level controls:
1. Volume vs 2025.
2. Revenue vs 2025.
3. Normative income vs 2025.
4. Project-wide run-rate forecast / potential.
5. Used agreed KU investment.
6. Recovery index vs pre-launch baseline.

Each card is tagged either `контур` or `проект` to prevent mixing filtered holding metrics with project-wide potential.

## Decision views
- Brand contribution waterfall: absolute bottle contribution to change vs comparable 2025.
- Brand decision matrix: volume change + KU change + shelf-price deviation -> commercial action.
- Shelf price table splits red price deviations into below-MRP and above-MRP risks.
